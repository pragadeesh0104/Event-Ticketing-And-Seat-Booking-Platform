# Eventify — User & Role Guide

## 1. Customer Guide
- **Account Registration**: Click "Register", select Customer role, enter name, email, and password.
- **Event Discovery**: Search by keyword or filter by category (Music, Tech, Sports, Arts).
- **Seat Booking**: Select an event, view the interactive seat map, pick available seats, and click "Reserve Seats".
- **Checkout & Payment**: Complete the 10-minute hold by entering simulated card details in the Demo Payment Gateway.
- **Digital Ticket Access**: View confirmed bookings in "My Bookings" and display/print your QR ticket.

## 2. Event Organizer Guide
- **Dashboard Overview**: Monitor total sales, tickets sold, venue occupancy, and revenue charts.
- **Create Event**: Add event title, description, category, poster URL, venue, start/end dates, and ticket prices.
- **Manage Bookings**: View customer booking records for your events and export reports as CSV.

## 3. Administrator Guide
- **User Governance**: View all platform accounts and toggle Active/Inactive statuses.
- **Event Moderation**: Review pending organizer events and approve/reject listings.
- **Ticket Scanner**: Input or scan QR verification tokens to validate tickets at venue gates.
- **Audit Logs**: Inspect platform activity logs for security and auditing.
